Tommy's started in May 2017 as a Knaresborough-born one-man shop. It grew the right way: through craft, consistency, familiar faces and a team that knows its customers by name.

Today the shop brings together classic barbering and modern men's styling in a relaxed space on Castlegate — professional where it matters, easygoing everywhere else.
